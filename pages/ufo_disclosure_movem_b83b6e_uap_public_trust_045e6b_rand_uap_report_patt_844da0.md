## Introduction

One of the most useful findings for understanding public [trust]({{ 'trust/' | relative_url }}) in UAP institutions does not come from a dramatic sighting or a classified file. It comes from a large statistical study of public reports. In 2023, RAND [researchers]({{ 'researchers/' | relative_url }}) analysed more than 101,000 UAP reports submitted across the United States between 1998 and 2022 and looked for geographic patterns. Their goal was not to determine whether any individual sighting was extraordinary, but to identify where reports were more likely to occur and what environmental factors might help explain them. The result was a striking pattern: reports were more common near military operations areas, suggesting that ordinary exposure to military aviation activity may play an important role in generating UAP reports. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[RAND Corporation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">RAND CorporationThe RAND Corporation, doing business as RAND, is an American nonprofit global policy think tank, research institute, a...</span></span></span>

For debates about UFO disclosure, this matters because it offers a data-driven explanation for at least part of the reporting landscape. Rather than assuming every cluster of sightings points to hidden technology or unexplained phenomena, the RAND analysis suggests that geography, visibility and public awareness of military activity can strongly influence what people report. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[RAND Corporation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">RAND CorporationThe RAND Corporation, doing business as RAND, is an American nonprofit global policy think tank, research institute, a...</span></span></span>

## What the public reports showed

RAND's study examined 101,151 reports from the National UFO Reporting Center (NUFORC), one of the most widely used public UFO-reporting databases in the United States. Researchers mapped reports across thousands of census-designated places and compared reporting rates with nearby infrastructure, including military operations areas, airports, weather stations and population density. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[RAND Corporation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">RAND CorporationThe RAND Corporation, doing business as RAND, is an American nonprofit global policy think tank, research institute, a...</span></span></span>

The most discussed finding was the relationship between military operations areas. The analysis found that public UAP reports were more likely when people were located near designated military airspace used for activities such as combat training, intercept exercises and low-altitude manoeuvres. RAND summarised the pattern as a consistent association between proximity to military operations areas and elevated reporting rates. Some reporting described the effect as roughly 1.2 times more likely within about 18 miles (30 kilometres) of such areas. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[RAND Corporation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">RAND CorporationThe RAND Corporation, doing business as RAND, is an American nonprofit global policy think tank, research institute, a...</span></span></span> <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.armytimes.com/news/your-military/2023/07/25/ufo-sightings-linked-to-military-training-locations-report-finds/?contentFeatureId=f0fmoahPVC2AbfL-2-1-8&amp;contentQuery=%7B%22includeSections%22%3A%22%2Fhome%22%2C%22excludeSections%22%3A%22%22%2C%22feedSize%22%3A10%2C%22feedOffset%22%3A1465%7D" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: armytimes.com">[Army Times]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">armytimes.com</span><span class="citation-popover-title">ufo sightings linked to military training locations report finds</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Using publicly available data from the National UFO Reporting Center, researchers...Read more...</span></span></span>

The military-area pattern does not mean military installations are generating unexplained phenomena. ### Why the pattern matters for public trust

The military connection is particularly significant because military facilities already occupy a central place in UFO culture. Many popular disclosure narratives focus on secret programmes, restricted airspace and classified technology.

When large numbers of public reports cluster near military activity, two competing interpretations become possible:

1. **Disclosure-oriented interpretation:** military areas attract reports because they are locations where unusual technologies are present.
2. **Operational interpretation:** military areas attract reports because civilians are observing legitimate military activities they do not recognise. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[RAND Corporation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">RAND CorporationThe RAND Corporation, doing business as RAND, is an American nonprofit global policy think tank, research institute, a...</span></span></span>

RAND's study does not settle that debate. ## What the military-area pattern does—and does not—prove

The strongest value of the RAND dataset is that it shifts attention from individual anecdotes to large-scale patterns. A database containing more than 100,000 reports cannot determine the nature of any single sighting, but it can reveal where reports tend to emerge and which environmental factors correlate with them. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/RAND_Corporation" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[RAND Corporation]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">RAND CorporationThe RAND Corporation, doing business as RAND, is an American nonprofit global policy think tank, research institute, a...</span></span></span>

The military-area pattern provides evidence that exposure to military aviation activity is associated with increased public reporting of UAPs. It does not demonstrate that military aircraft explain every report, nor does it prove that all sightings near military areas are misidentifications.     if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId) < 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket) < 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
