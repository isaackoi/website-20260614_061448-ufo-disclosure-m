## Why Congress Ordered a Historical Review

AARO's historical review was not an isolated Pentagon initiative. Congress directed AARO to investigate historical claims concerning unidentified anomalous phenomena (UAP), alleged crash-retrieval programmes and claims of hidden government projects. The office gathered archival records, interviewed current and former officials, reviewed classified and unclassified materials, and examined allegations submitted through newly established reporting channels. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/article/3575588/aaro-director-dr-sean-kirkpatrick-holds-an-off-camera-media-roundtable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: war.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">war.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">aaro director dr sean kirkpatrick holds an off camera media roundtable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Department of WarAARO Director Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick Holds an Off-Camera...31 Oct 2023 — These reports will be used to inform AARO&#x27;s cong...</span></span></span>

The review was intended to answer a question that had become increasingly important after the 2017 revelations: did the US government possess evidence of non-human technology, and did earlier Pentagon programmes support such claims? The report's first volume examined government involvement with UAP from 1945 through 2023 and specifically addressed the history of AAWSAP and AATIP. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://theelectricgf.com/2024/03/13/pentagon-report-finds-no-evidence-of-extraterrestrial-activity/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: theelectricgf.com">[The Electric]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">theelectricgf.com</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">The ElectricPentagon report finds no evidence of extraterrestrial activityMar 13, 2024 — The March 8 report is the first volume of AARO&#x27;s...</span></span></span>

By the time AARO began its investigation, the public understanding of AATIP had expanded far beyond the original reporting. Books, documentaries, congressional [hearings]({{ 'hearings/' | relative_url }}) and whistle-blower allegations had created an impression that AATIP represented a dedicated government effort uncovering extraordinary aerospace capabilities. AARO's task was to determine whether the documentary record supported that interpretation. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/article/3575588/aaro-director-dr-sean-kirkpatrick-holds-an-off-camera-media-roundtable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: war.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">war.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">aaro director dr sean kirkpatrick holds an off camera media roundtable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Department of WarAARO Director Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick Holds an Off-Camera...31 Oct 2023 — These reports will be used to inform AARO&#x27;s cong...</span></span></span>

## How AARO Reframed the Relationship Between AAWSAP and AATIP

The most consequential finding concerned the relationship between AAWSAP and AATIP.

Following the 2017 reporting, many readers understood AATIP to be the principal Pentagon UFO programme. AARO argued that this characterisation oversimplified the historical record. According to the review, AAWSAP was the formally funded Defence Intelligence Agency programme created in 2008 and operated through a contract awarded to Bigelow Aerospace Advanced Space Studies. AAWSAP received the programme funding and generated the bulk of the documented research output. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/article/3575588/aaro-director-dr-sean-kirkpatrick-holds-an-off-camera-media-roundtable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: war.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">war.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">aaro director dr sean kirkpatrick holds an off camera media roundtable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Department of WarAARO Director Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick Holds an Off-Camera...31 Oct 2023 — These reports will be used to inform AARO&#x27;s cong...</span></span></span>

AARO concluded that the name "AATIP" later acquired a public prominence disproportionate to its documented bureaucratic status. One of the report's most discussed statements was its conclusion that AATIP was not established as a formally recognised Department of Defense programme in the same way AAWSAP was. The report stated that after AAWSAP ended, some personnel continued discussing UAP-related issues and used the AATIP label within an informal community of interest, but the surviving records did not support the notion of a distinct, officially funded successor programme operating at the scale often implied in public discussions. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/article/3575588/aaro-director-dr-sean-kirkpatrick-holds-an-off-camera-media-roundtable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: war.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">war.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">aaro director dr sean kirkpatrick holds an off camera media roundtable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Department of WarAARO Director Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick Holds an Off-Camera...31 Oct 2023 — These reports will be used to inform AARO&#x27;s cong...</span></span></span>

This finding directly affected how the 2017 story is interpreted. The original reporting was factually correct that government personnel investigated unusual reports and that UAP-related work existed. However, AARO argued that the public narrative gradually compressed AAWSAP, AATIP, contractor research activities and later informal efforts into a single programme history. The review's central correction was therefore not that AATIP never existed in any sense, but that its role had often been described in ways that blurred important distinctions. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/article/3575588/aaro-director-dr-sean-kirkpatrick-holds-an-off-camera-media-roundtable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: war.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">war.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">aaro director dr sean kirkpatrick holds an off camera media roundtable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Department of WarAARO Director Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick Holds an Off-Camera...31 Oct 2023 — These reports will be used to inform AARO&#x27;s cong...</span></span></span>

## What AAWSAP Was Actually Studying

AARO's review also challenged assumptions about what the programme was primarily doing.

Public discussion often portrayed the programme as focused on investigating military UFO encounters. The review found that AAWSAP's contracted work extended much more broadly into speculative aerospace and frontier-science subjects. [Researchers]({{ 'researchers/' | relative_url }}) produced a series of Defence Intelligence Reference Documents (DIRDs) examining topics ranging from advanced propulsion concepts and exotic physics to highly theoretical aerospace technologies. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Index%3AAARO_Historical_Record_Report_Volume_1_2024.pdf" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: en.wikisource.org">[Wikisource]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">en.wikisource.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Index:AARO Historical Record Report Volume 1 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Index:AARO Historical Record Report Volume 1 2024.pdf11 May 2024 — Advanced Aerospace Weapons System Application Program (AAWSA...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2024</span></span></span>

AARO noted that these papers were exploratory studies rather than validated scientific breakthroughs. The report specifically stated that the scientific papers generated under the contract were never thoroughly peer reviewed. As a result, their existence did not constitute evidence that the government had verified extraordinary technological claims. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3AAARO_Historical_Record_Report_Volume_1_2024.pdf/23" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: en.wikisource.org">[Wikisource]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">en.wikisource.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Page:AARO Historical Record Report Volume 1 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Page:AARO Historical Record Report Volume 1 2024.pdf/235 May 2024 — Results: The AAWSAP/AATIP contract with the private sector...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2024</span></span></span>

The review further reported that some contractor-associated activities expanded beyond the programme's formal research objectives. AARO described investigations involving paranormal and UAP-related subjects at property owned by the contractor organisation, activities that extended beyond the official statement of work. This finding reinforced the office's argument that later public accounts often merged formal government research with broader interests pursued by individuals associated with the programme. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://en.wikisource.org/wiki/Page%3AAARO_Historical_Record_Report_Volume_1_2024.pdf/34" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: en.wikisource.org">[Wikisource]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">en.wikisource.org</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Page:AARO Historical Record Report Volume 1 2024</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Page:AARO Historical Record Report Volume 1 2024.pdf/346 May 2024 — As discussed in Section IV of this report, while the offici...</span><span class="citation-popover-meta">Published: May 2024</span></span></span>

For historians of the disclosure movement, this distinction is important because it suggests that some of the mythology surrounding AATIP originated not from official findings but from the wider intellectual environment surrounding AAWSAP and its [contractors]({{ 'contractors/' | relative_url }}).

## Why Recovered Technology Claims Were Not Supported

The most controversial portion of the review addressed allegations that the US government possessed recovered non-human technology.

For years, disclosure advocates had argued that crash-retrieval programmes operated within highly classified compartments of government and industry. These allegations gained renewed attention after former intelligence officer David Grusch publicly claimed that the government possessed retrieved craft of non-human origin.

AARO reviewed historical records, programme files and testimony related to such allegations. Its conclusion was unambiguous: investigators found no empirical evidence that any US government investigation had confirmed extraterrestrial technology, recovered alien spacecraft or reverse-engineering programmes hidden from Congress. ## The Kona Blue Example and What It Revealed

One of the report's most revealing case studies involved a proposed initiative called KONA BLUE.

AARO disclosed that government officials had considered creating a special access programme intended to investigate claims involving advanced aerospace technology and alleged non-human materials. The proposal reflected the genuine belief among some advocates that the government possessed hidden technology. However, according to AARO, the proposal was rejected by Department of Homeland Security leadership because the underlying claims lacked evidentiary support. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-domain_Anomaly_Resolution_Office" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office</span></span></span>

The significance of KONA BLUE was not that it demonstrated the existence of recovered alien craft. Rather, it illustrated how belief in such programmes existed within portions of government even when evidence remained unverified. For AARO, the episode showed how sincere conviction could contribute to the persistence of extraordinary narratives without necessarily establishing their factual basis. <span class="citation-chip-wrap"><a class="citation-chip" href="https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/All-domain_Anomaly_Resolution_Office" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: Wikipedia">[Wikipedia]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">Wikipedia</span><span class="citation-popover-title">All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">All-domain Anomaly Resolution Office</span></span></span>

This example became central to the report's broader thesis: many influential claims in UFO culture can be traced through networks of officials, contractors and researchers who genuinely believed extraordinary stories but lacked documentary proof sufficient to withstand historical scrutiny.

## What the Review Changed About the 2017 Narrative

The lasting importance of AARO's review lies in how it reshaped understanding of the 2017 revelations.

The original reporting transformed UFOs from a fringe topic into a mainstream policy issue involving military personnel, aviation safety and intelligence oversight. AARO did not reverse that shift. The office continues to investigate unexplained reports and acknowledges that some UAP cases remain unresolved because available data are insufficient for definitive conclusions. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.aaro.mil/Congressional-Press-Products/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: aaro.mil">[AARO]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">aaro.mil</span><span class="citation-popover-title">Congressional Press Products</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Congressional/Press Products03/08/2024, PRESS RELEASES, Statement by Pentagon Press Secretary Maj. Gen. Pat Ryder on the Historical R...</span></span></span>

What the review challenged was a different proposition: that the existence of AAWSAP or AATIP demonstrated hidden government knowledge of non-human technology. AARO argued that the historical record instead showed a mixture of exploratory research, bureaucratic ambiguity, speculative studies, informal communities of interest and recurring but unverified extraordinary claims. <span class="citation-link-wrap"><a class="citation-inline-link" href="https://www.war.gov/News/Transcripts/Transcript/article/3575588/aaro-director-dr-sean-kirkpatrick-holds-an-off-camera-media-roundtable/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer nofollow" aria-label="View source: war.gov">[U.S. Department of War]</a><span class="citation-popover" role="note"><span class="citation-popover-source">war.gov</span><span class="citation-popover-title">aaro director dr sean kirkpatrick holds an off camera media roundtable</span><span class="citation-popover-snippet">Department of WarAARO Director Dr. Sean Kirkpatrick Holds an Off-Camera...31 Oct 2023 — These reports will be used to inform AARO&#x27;s cong...</span></span></span>

As a result, the 2024 review introduced a major correction into the history of the disclosure movement. The key question shifted from whether the Pentagon once had a UFO programme to how that programme was actually structured and what it genuinely discovered. AARO's answer was that the documentary record supports a far more limited and complicated story than the one that became widely accepted after 2017. Whether critics ultimately accept that conclusion or continue to challenge it, the review fundamentally altered the historical debate by separating programme mythology from what investigators believe can be demonstrated through surviving government evidence.     return url + joiner + params.join('&');
  }
  function domainLabel(marketId) {
    var cfg = markets[marketId] || markets[defaultMarket] || {};
    return cfg.domain_label || 'eBay';
  }
  function flagClass(marketId) {
    return 'fr-ebay-market-flag fr-ebay-market-flag--' + String(marketId || '').toLowerCase().replace(/_/g, '-').replace(/[^a-z0-9-]+/g, '');
  }
  function closeMenu(section) {
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var picker = section.querySelector('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
    if (menu) menu.hidden = true;
    if (trigger) trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', 'false');
    if (picker) picker.classList.remove('fr-ebay-market-picker--open');
  }
  function availableMarkets(section) {
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      return Array.prototype.slice.call(select.options)
        .map(function (option) { return option.value; })
        .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
    }
    return Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]'))
      .map(function (button) { return button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'); })
      .filter(function (marketId) { return !!markets[marketId]; });
  }
  function applyMarket(section, marketId, persist) {
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    if (available.indexOf(marketId) < 0) marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-localized-link]')).forEach(function (link) {
      var query = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-query') || '';
      var reference = link.getAttribute('data-ebay-reference') || '';
      link.setAttribute('href', buildUrl(query, reference, marketId));
    });
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select && available.indexOf(marketId) >= 0) select.value = marketId;
    var selectedText = marketId;
    if (select && select.selectedIndex >= 0) selectedText = select.options[select.selectedIndex].text;
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-selected-market-flag], [data-ebay-trigger-market-flag]')).forEach(function (flag) {
      flag.className = flagClass(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-trigger-market-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = selectedText;
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-domain-label]')).forEach(function (label) {
      label.textContent = domainLabel(marketId);
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.setAttribute('aria-selected', button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option') === marketId ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      var active = button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button') === marketId;
      button.setAttribute('aria-pressed', active ? 'true' : 'false');
    });
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-selected-market', marketId);
    if (persist) {
      try { if (window.localStorage) localStorage.setItem('phoenix-ebay-market', marketId); } catch (err) {}
    }
    closeMenu(section);
  }
  sections.forEach(function (section) {
    if (section.getAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init') === '1') return;
    section.setAttribute('data-ebay-localized-init', '1');
    var available = availableMarkets(section);
    var marketId = defaultMarket;
    if (window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation && window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket) {
      marketId = window.PhoenixAffiliateLocation.inferMarket(available, {
        storageKey: 'phoenix-ebay-market',
        defaultMarket: defaultMarket
      });
    } else if (available.indexOf(defaultMarket) < 0) {
      marketId = available[0] || defaultMarket;
    }
    var select = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-select]');
    if (select) {
      select.addEventListener('change', function () {
        applyMarket(section, select.value, true);
      });
    }
    var trigger = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-trigger]');
    var menu = section.querySelector('[data-ebay-market-menu]');
    if (trigger && menu) {
      trigger.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
        event.stopPropagation();
        var expanded = trigger.getAttribute('aria-expanded') === 'true';
        menu.hidden = expanded;
        trigger.setAttribute('aria-expanded', expanded ? 'false' : 'true');
        var picker = trigger.closest('.fr-ebay-market-picker');
        if (picker) picker.classList.toggle('fr-ebay-market-picker--open', !expanded);
      });
      Array.prototype.slice.call(menu.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-option]')).forEach(function (button) {
        button.addEventListener('click', function (event) {
          event.stopPropagation();
          applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-option'), true);
        });
      });
      document.addEventListener('click', function () { closeMenu(section); });
    }
    Array.prototype.slice.call(section.querySelectorAll('[data-ebay-market-button]')).forEach(function (button) {
      button.addEventListener('click', function () {
        applyMarket(section, button.getAttribute('data-ebay-market-button'), true);
      });
    });
    applyMarket(section, marketId, false);
  });
})();
