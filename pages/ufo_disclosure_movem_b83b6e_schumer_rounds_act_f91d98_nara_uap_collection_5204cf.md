## How the collection was created

The final 2024 law required NARA to establish the “Unidentified Anomalous Phenomena Records Collection” and directed agencies to review, identify, and organise UAP records in their custody. This matters because archival structure affects public visibility. Before Record Group 615, records relating to UFOs or UAPs could be dispersed across military archives, intelligence repositories, aviation records, scientific agencies, and departmental filing systems. ## The lasting significance of Record Group 615
